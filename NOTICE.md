# Third-party source and attribution

## International Personality Item Pool (IPIP)

The original English questionnaire items and scale definitions used by this
repository are derived from the International Personality Item Pool (IPIP).

Official site:

- https://ipip.ori.org/
- https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm
- https://ipip.ori.org/newhexaco_pi_key.htm

The IPIP states that its items, scales, and inventories are in the public
domain and may be copied, edited, translated, and used for any purpose,
commercial or non-commercial, without requesting permission.

This repository does not claim copyright over the original English IPIP item
texts.

## IPIP-HEXACO reference

Ashton, M. C., Lee, K., & Goldberg, L. R. (2007).
The IPIP-HEXACO scales: An alternative, public-domain measure of the
personality constructs in the HEXACO model.
Personality and Individual Differences, 42, 1515-1526.

## Japanese translation in this repository

The Japanese translations, explanatory notes, questionnaire UI, scoring
implementation, comparison tools, and documentation in this repository are
independent work and are distributed under the repository's MIT License.

The Japanese translation is not an official Japanese HEXACO-PI-R and has not
been psychometrically standardized or validated as an equivalent Japanese
instrument. Translation choices intentionally favor understandable Japanese
and preservation of item intent over word-for-word literal translation.
