# Translation review

## Policy

All 240 IPIP-HEXACO items were checked one-by-one against the English source on 2026-10-06.

The review used these rules:

- Preserve the behavioral or attitudinal meaning of the English item.
- Prefer natural Japanese over word-for-word literal translation.
- Do not add conditions that are absent from the source item.
- Put interpretation help in `guidance`, not in the scored statement, when extra context would otherwise bias the answer.
- Avoid narrowing a broad English item merely because its parent facet suggests one interpretation.
- Keep reversed items semantically reversed; do not rewrite them into the positive direction.
- When an English idiom is opaque in Japanese, translate the intended meaning and preserve the original English text alongside it.
